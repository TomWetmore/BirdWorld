I. Additions.
   a. Valid Species and Subspecies which have been described as new, revived, or recorded as North American.

42.1
Species: Larus barroviansi: Ridgw. Point Barrow Gull
Larus barroviansis Ridgw, Auk, III. July, 1886, 330.
Habitat: Bering's Sea and contiguous waters: northeastward to Point Barrow, southwestward to Japan.

[60.1]
Species: Larus minutus: Pall.: Little Gull
Larus minutus Pall., Reis. Russ. Reichs. III. App. No. 35, 1771, 702.
Habitat: Europe and parts of Asia and Africa; accidental in the Bermudas and on Long Island. (Cf. Dutcher, Auk. V. April 1888, 171.)
